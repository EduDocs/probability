# Sums — scratch

> Scratch/ideas only. The shipped prose lives in `appendix.tex`. Not part of the LaTeX build.

## Purpose
Collect a small appendix of summation identities used elsewhere in the notes (arithmetic, square, and cubic sums), with one worked derivation as a representative proof.

## Key points / outline
- Sample expectation manipulation reused as a motivating display (E[Y] for Y = g(X) via grouping over preimages).
- Closed form for sum_{k=1}^n k = n(n+1)/2, with a pairing-style proof.
- Closed form for sum_{k=1}^n k^2 = n(n+1)(2n+1)/6 (stated, proof omitted).
- Closed form for sum_{k=1}^n k^3 = n^2(n+1)^2/4 (stated).

## Open questions
- The chapter is marked `\chapter{Sums}` but is listed in the manuscript as an appendix; the title may want renaming, and the opening E[Y] display reads as orphaned context from another chapter.
- Only the linear sum has a proof; the square and cubic identities are stated bare — possibly worth adding short derivations or pointers.
